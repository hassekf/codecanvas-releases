## Installing

Download the `.dmg`, open it and drag CanvasCode into your Applications folder.

The app is signed and notarised by Apple. It opens the first time with no unidentified-developer
warning, no right-click workaround and no approval in System Settings.

## What the Mac needs

- **macOS 26.1** or newer.
- Nothing else to use the canvas: opening panels, browsing, arranging and writing notes.

Two things are optional and can come later:

- **The agent program.** CanvasCode installs Claude Code in one click, with no administrator
  password, from the first agent's panel. See [the first agent](primeiro-agente).
- **An OpenAI key**, for voice and for generating images. Without it, the rest of the app works the
  same. See [setting up voice](configurar-a-voz).

## Updating

The app looks for new versions on its own, shows what changed, and replaces itself when you accept.

- To check right away: the **CanvasCode → Check for Updates…** menu, or the **Check for Updates**
  button at the bottom of Settings.
- Installing a new version quits the app, and with it the agents that were working. The warning comes
  first, with the version number.

The detail is in [updates](atualizacoes).

## Where the data lives

On your machine. Projects, panels, notes and agent conversations stay on your computer, and the app
does not require an account to work. See [where my data lives](onde-ficam-meus-dados).

## If the Mac says the file is damaged

The download came through incomplete. Download it again. If it persists, see
[when something doesn't work](quando-algo-nao-funciona).
